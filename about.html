About

Built for the space between information and action

Pactura is proprietary AI workflow intelligence technology developed by Aurum Equity LLC.

Why Pactura exists

Organizations receive more information than people can sort by hand

  • Information overload

    Requests, documents, and messages arrive faster than teams can read, classify, and route them.

  • Fragmented systems

    Information lives across inboxes, forms, and applications that do not share context.

  • Manual decisions

    People make the same judgment calls repeatedly, with little record of how they were made.

Pactura was designed to create an intelligence layer between incoming information and organizational action, with governance built in from the start.

Mission

Automation should stay accountable to the people it affects

Automated systems increasingly shape decisions about hiring, lending, housing, insurance, and benefits. When they get it wrong, the cost falls hardest on people with the least power to contest it.

That is why Pactura is built to support human judgment rather than replace it. Review points, cited answers, and audit trails are part of the product, not add-ons.

Pactura’s founder, a software engineer in Birmingham, Alabama, has navigated these systems firsthand, from credit reporting errors to insurance disputes. That experience shapes a simple conviction. Opacity in automated systems hurts most when people have no institutional backing.

If you work with communities affected by automated decisions, we would like to hear from you at info@pactura.ai.
