What Pactura does

Pactura.ai is built for legal, healthcare, financial, government, and other regulated environments. The platform helps organizations identify sensitive information, extract critical data, automate document review, and maintain human oversight across high-stakes workflows.

Every output stays traceable back to its source document, with confidence scoring and audit history attached at every step. Pactura integrates with the systems already in place rather than requiring new ones.

How it works

Intake to structured output, with a person in the loop

Your documents stay wherever they already live. We plug in around them.

01

Intake

Documents arrive through your existing storage, email, CRM, or case-management system, so there's no new system for your team to learn.

02

AI processing

Our engine ingests, classifies, and extracts structured data from every file, scoring confidence at every field.

03

Human review

Anything below a set confidence threshold routes to a reviewer, keeping a person accountable for every high-stakes decision.

04

Structured output

Clean, tagged records land in your database, CRM, or case-management system of choice, with a confidence score and source trail attached.
