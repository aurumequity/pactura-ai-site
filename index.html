How it works

From incoming information to governed action

One model for every workflow, with each step designed to stay visible, reviewable, and traceable.

  1. 01

    Receive

    Forms, files, requests, inquiries, messages, and other incoming information.

  2. 02

    Understand

    Identify meaning, intent, context, and key details.

  3. 03

    Decide

    Apply organizational rules and workflow logic.

  4. 04

    Transform

    Convert unstructured information into structured outputs.

  5. 05

    Route

    Send the result to the appropriate person, team, or system.

  6. 06

    Audit

    Maintain traceability, review points, and governance.

Human oversight

AI with people still in control

Pactura is designed to support decisions, not make them alone. People review what the system finds, decide what happens next, and keep a record of it.

  • Structured outputs

    Results arrive as findings people can inspect, not open-ended answers.

  • People decide

    Findings become remediation tasks that people track to resolution.

  • Answers with sources

    Document chat responses cite the passages they draw from.

  • Traceable by default

    Uploads, analyses, and questions are recorded in audit logs.
