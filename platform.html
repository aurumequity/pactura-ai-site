Platform

One intelligence layer for incoming information

Pactura reads what comes in, applies your rules, and hands structured results to people and systems. Each capability below shows what works today and what the platform is designed to do.

The model

Receive, understand, decide, transform, route, audit

Every workflow follows the same six steps. That keeps results consistent and every decision traceable.

Current product capability

Works in Pactura today.

Workflow pattern

A workflow Pactura is designed for. It shows the intended model, not a live feature, and depends on routing, connectors, or intake that are not available yet.

Capabilities

What each part of the platform does

  1. Receive

    Universal intake

    Bring incoming information into one governed workspace instead of scattered inboxes and folders.

    Current product capability

    Document upload with version history, inside organization workspaces with role-based access.

    Workflow pattern

    Forms, email, inquiries, and requests arriving from other systems.

  2. Understand

    AI understanding

    Read incoming information for meaning, context, and the details that matter.

    Current product capability

    Document chat that answers questions with citations to the source passages. Anomaly detection that flags unusual clauses, missing clauses, conflicting terms, and non-standard language, rated by severity.

    Workflow pattern

    Identifying intent, topic, and urgency for any incoming item, not only documents.

  3. Transform

    Structured extraction

    Turn unstructured content into consistent, structured outputs people can review and reuse.

    Current product capability

    Gap checks return structured findings, each with a requirement, a status of met, partial, or missing, supporting evidence, and a recommendation. Version comparison shows what changed between document versions.

    Workflow pattern

    Extracting the fields your organization defines into structured records.

  4. Decide

    Decision and classification

    Apply rules consistently so similar items are handled the same way.

    Current product capability

    Gap checks evaluate documents against SOC 2, HIPAA, GDPR, CMMC, and FINRA criteria.

    Workflow pattern

    Applying your own organizational rules, classifications, and priorities.

  5. Route

    Routing

    Deliver each result to the person, team, or system that should act on it.

    Not available yet

    No part of this step is live today.

    Workflow pattern

    Sending results to owners, queues, and downstream systems automatically. Today, findings can be turned into remediation tasks for manual follow-up.

  6. Review

    Human review

    Keep people in control of decisions, especially ones with external or regulatory consequences.

    Current product capability

    People review findings and track remediation tasks to resolution. Role-based access controls who can see and change what.

    Workflow pattern

    Configurable approval points before any result leaves Pactura.

  7. Audit

    Auditability

    Keep a record of what came in, what was found, and who acted.

    Current product capability

    Audit logs of uploads, analyses, and questions. Audit summaries of a document’s compliance posture. Evidence packages exported as PDF.

    Workflow pattern

    End-to-end traceability from an incoming item to the action taken on it.

  8. Connect

    Existing-system integration

    Work between the tools you already run instead of replacing them.

    Current product capability

    Results leave Pactura as exports, such as evidence packages.

    Workflow pattern

    Connectors to CRMs, databases, document repositories, workflow automation platforms, and internal applications.

Positioning

A layer between systems, not a replacement for them

Workflow automation tools move data between steps. CRMs and document systems store it. Pactura is designed to add the judgment in between, reading each item, deciding what it is and where it belongs, and passing a structured result along.

You keep the systems your teams already know.
