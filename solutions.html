Workflow pattern

A workflow Pactura is designed for. It shows the intended model, not a live feature, and depends on routing, connectors, or intake that are not available yet.

  1. Workflow pattern

    Reduce manual intake

    The challengeStaff re-key the same details from emails, forms, and files into other systems.

    The approachRead each incoming item once and produce a structured record, so people review instead of retype.

  2. Workflow pattern

    Standardize decision-making

    The challengeSimilar requests are handled differently depending on who picks them up.

    The approachApply the same organizational rules to every item and surface exceptions for a person to decide.

  3. Workflow pattern

    Improve routing

    The challengeRequests wait in shared inboxes until someone notices them.

    The approachSend each result to the owner, team, or system that should act on it.

  4. Workflow pattern

    Create structured operational data

    The challengeValuable information stays locked inside documents and messages.

    The approachTurn it into consistent fields you can report on and reuse. Today this applies to compliance findings.

  5. Current product capability

    Preserve human oversight

    The challengeAutomation that acts on its own creates risk in regulated and public-facing work.

    The approachPeople review findings and track remediation tasks to resolution, with role-based access controlling who can change what.

  6. Current product capability

    Strengthen traceability

    The challengeIt is hard to show later what was reviewed and why a decision was made.

    The approachAudit logs record uploads, analyses, and questions. Audit summaries and exported evidence packages document the result.

  7. Workflow pattern

    Connect disconnected workflows

    The challengeInformation moves between tools by copy and paste.

    The approachAct as the intelligence layer between the systems you already use rather than replacing them.
