Use cases

Where Pactura fits

Four workflows, organized around outcomes. Compliance runs on current product capabilities. The others are workflow patterns that show how Pactura is designed to work.

Current product capability

Works in Pactura today.

Workflow pattern

A workflow Pactura is designed for. It shows the intended model, not a live feature, and depends on routing, connectors, or intake that are not available yet.

Workflow pattern

Marketing intelligence

Inbound interest arrives through forms, email, and referrals. Pactura is designed to classify each lead, identify intent, summarize the request, set a priority, and route it into the systems your team already uses.

An example. A healthcare organization asks about a public-awareness campaign. The pattern turns that message into a structured record your team can act on.

Workflow pattern

Public relations

Media inquiries need fast, careful handling. Pactura is designed to identify the inquiry, its topic, urgency, deadline, and potential risk, then recommend an owner.

People stay in control of every response. Pactura does not reply to reporters. It prepares structured information so the right person can decide what to say.

Workflow pattern

Government operations

Public-sector teams handle high volumes of incoming information with strict expectations for accuracy and accountability. Pactura is designed to structure that intake and keep a clear record of how each item was handled.

Workflows this pattern is designed for include

  • Stakeholder and constituent correspondence
  • Public affairs intake
  • Procurement and grant-related intake
  • Public comments
  • Administrative request workflows
Current product capability

Compliance and document review

This is what Pactura does today. Upload a document, check it against a framework, and get structured findings your team can act on and trace.

  • Gap checks against SOC 2, HIPAA, GDPR, CMMC, and FINRA criteria
  • Anomaly detection for unusual, missing, or conflicting terms
  • Version comparison between document revisions
  • Document chat with cited answers
  • Remediation tasks, audit summaries, and audit logs
  • Evidence packages exported as PDF

Gap checks are designed to support qualified review, not replace it.
